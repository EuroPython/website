## When

Tuesday, 14 July 2026 — Evening *(exact time to be announced)*

## Where

A venue in Kraków, Poland. Venue details will be announced closer to the event.

## Who

The Speakers' Dinner is exclusively for accepted speakers at EuroPython 2026. Speakers will receive an invitation with all details via email.

## About the Dinner

The Speakers' Dinner is a chance for all speakers to connect, relax, and celebrate together before the conference begins. You'll meet fellow speakers, programme team members, and EPS board members in an informal and welcoming atmosphere.

The dinner includes a multi-course meal with vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options, plus beverages. All dietary requirements will be accommodated — please indicate yours when registering.

More details, including the venue address and exact timings, will be shared with speakers closer to the event. Watch your email and our [blog](https://blog.europython.eu/) for announcements.
