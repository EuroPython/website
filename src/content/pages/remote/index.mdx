# EuroPython 2026 Remote

As happy as we are about seeing many friends in [Kraków](/tips)
this year, we are also aware that many of you will not be able to make it in person.
That’s why EuroPython 2026 Remote is back.
Wherever you are, let's celebrate Python and our community, rejoice at our diversity,
learn and have fun in Kraków and beyond! In this spirit, we strive to make the remote participation
as engaging as possible.
<Image src={remote} width="1200" alt="" />

## What is included in the remote ticket?
Remote participation between 15-17 July (Wednesday - Friday):
- Livestreams of Keynotes, panel discussions, all 6 tracks of talks.
- Live text-based participation in post-talk Q&A sessions.
- Text-based discussions with speakers, where available.
- Online job board and chat with sponsors at the remote booths.
- On-demand viewing, which will be available hours after the livestream.
- Access to our conference Discord server.

<Note type="warning">Tutorials and Workshops on 13 and 14 July will not be streamed.</Note>

## Remote participation access

<Note>Remote participation (livestream and online platform access) is included with both **in-person and remote tickets**.</Note>
Access to the conference livestreams (15–17 July) is included as part of the remote ticket.
We continue to offer remote tickets to ensure that EuroPython remains accessible
while also helping to support the running of the conference and the wider community.

If you would like to watch EuroPython remotely, please consider purchasing a remote ticket.
This not only gives you access to the conference but also helps support the community.
