## General Enquiries

For general questions about the conference, tickets, or anything else:
[helpdesk@europython.eu](mailto:helpdesk@europython.eu)

## Sponsorship

Interested in sponsoring EuroPython 2026?
[sponsoring@europython.eu](mailto:sponsoring@europython.eu)

## Financial Aid

Questions about grants, visa fee reimbursement, or the financial aid programme:
[finaid@europython.eu](mailto:finaid@europython.eu)

## Visa Support

Need an invitation letter or have questions about your visa application:
[visa@europython.eu](mailto:visa@europython.eu)

## Code of Conduct

To report a CoC incident or get in touch with our CoC committee:
[coc@europython.eu](mailto:coc@europython.eu)

## EPS Board

To contact the EuroPython Society board directly:
[board@europython-society.org](mailto:board@europython-society.org)

## Follow us

Stay up to date with EuroPython news and announcements:

- [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/europython)
- [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@EuroPythonConference)
- [Mastodon](https://fosstodon.org/@europython)

Subscribe to our [blog and newsletter](https://blog.europython.eu/) for the latest updates.

## Website & Privacy

Found a bug on our website? [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/EuroPython/website).

For privacy-related enquiries: [privacy@europython.eu](mailto:privacy@europython.eu)
