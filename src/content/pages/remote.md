## What's included with a remote ticket

- Live streams of keynotes and all six talk tracks (Wednesday–Friday, 15–17 July)
- Live Q&A participation via Discord
- Access to the EuroPython Discord community during and after the event
- Job board access
- Video archive access after the event

**Note:** Tutorials (Monday–Tuesday, 13–14 July) and Sprints (Saturday–Sunday, 18–19 July) are not streamed. A remote ticket covers the main conference days only.

## Live streams

Watch keynotes and all talk tracks live as they happen. All recorded talks are published to the [EuroPython YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@EuroPythonConference) after the event.

## Live Q&A

Participate in Q&A sessions with speakers via our Discord community. Remote attendees are fully integrated into the Q&A experience.

## Remote ticket pricing

Remote tickets are significantly lower in price than in-person tickets. Check our [tickets page](/participate/#tickets) for current pricing.

Financial aid (including free remote tickets) is available for those who need it. See our [financial aid programme](/participate/#finaid).
