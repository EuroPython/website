## What to expect

EuroPython's social event is a beloved tradition — a chance to step away from the sessions and enjoy an evening with the community. Past events have featured live music, board games, outdoor spaces, food and drinks for all dietary preferences, and much more.

EuroPython 2026 is in Kraków, one of Europe's most vibrant and beautiful cities. We're planning an evening that does justice to the setting.

The social event is designed to be welcoming and inclusive. Quiet spaces will be available, along with food and drink options for all dietary requirements including vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free.
