## Conference Venue - Prague Congress Centre

[The Prague Congress Centre](https://www.praguecc.cz/en/homepage) will host
EuroPython 2025 from **July 14th to July 20th**. It’s a renowned venue, having
hosted the IMF, NATO, and other significant international organizations.

The conference centre is adjacent to Vyšehrad—just a short walk from
[Náplavka](https://www.mmzoneblog.com/naplavka), a lively area filled with
cafes, pubs, and restaurants, perfect for relaxing by the river and soaking up
the city’s spirit.

Vyšehrad station (on Line C of the Prague underground railway) is right next to
the venue and offers convenient public transport from the airport and all major
rail and bus stations.
