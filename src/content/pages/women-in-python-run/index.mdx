# Women in Python 5k Run 🏃‍♀️

Lace up and join us for a relaxed, inclusive 5k run by the river! This one is
about community and fun, not competition — all paces are welcome, whether you
run, jog, or walk. Join us and have fun!

![The Wisła (Vistula) river winding through Kraków on a bright summer day](./wisla-krakow-morning.webp)

_Photo: [Maksym Kozlenko](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2017-05-29_Vistula_in_Krak%C3%B3w.jpg), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), via Wikimedia Commons._

## Who is it for?

Everyone identifying as FLINTA (women, lesbian, intersex, non-binary, trans, and
agender people) is warmly welcome to join.

## When & Where?

- 🗓 **When:** Thursday morning
- 📍 **Where:** By the river (exact meeting point to be announced)
- 💸 **Cost:** Free — join us and have fun!

## Tickets

A ticket voucher will be shared with registered attendees. If you haven't
registered for the run yet, you can add it to your ticket as usual through the
ticket shop.
