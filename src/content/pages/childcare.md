## Who is it for?

Free childcare is available for children aged **19 months and older**. Children under 19 months are welcome to attend with their own caregiver.

## When is it available?

Childcare runs during the main conference days (Wednesday–Friday, 15–17 July 2026). Full hours and room location will be announced closer to the event.

## How to apply

Childcare must be requested during ticket registration. If you've already registered and need to add childcare, contact [helpdesk@europython.eu](mailto:helpdesk@europython.eu).

## About the childcare service

Our childcare service is provided by professional childcare staff. The childcare room is located within the ICE Kraków Congress Centre, close to the main conference areas, so you're never far from your child.

The environment is safe, stimulating, and child-friendly, with age-appropriate activities and materials. Staff-to-child ratios meet regulatory requirements.

Childcare is provided free of charge as part of EuroPython's commitment to making the conference accessible to parents and carers.
