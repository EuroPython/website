# Speakers' Dinner
We are excited to announce a special Speakers' Dinner! This exclusive event is for speakers only, providing an opportunity to meet and mingle before the main conference days.

The dinner will feature multiple courses, and you can choose from a variety of alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages. We also offer a variety of options to accommodate different dietary preferences, including vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free.

## Where?
The Speakers' Dinner will take place at [Panorama Forum](https://www.forumpanorama.pl/en), featuring the industrial architecture of the former Hotel Forum and a beautiful view of the Kraków skyline.

Address:
<address>
ul. Marii Konopnickiej 28, Kraków <br/>
6th floor · Hotel Forum <br/>
</address>

## When?
On Tuesday July 14th at 7.30 p.m.

## How to get there?
* The former Hotel Forum building is just a [5 - 10 minutes walk](https://maps.app.goo.gl/fhb2sRh6J8i3RT5m8) from the ICE Kraków.
