  Optiver's Production Engineering teams manage our live trading environment, active across 50+ global exchanges and hundreds of thousands of interconnected financial products. Our infrastructure is a combination of vastly distributed systems, high-performance computing, low-latency trading algorithms, and high-throughput dataflows. We take an in-house approach to building, analysing, managing and improving our custom infrastructure – our data lives at our own data centre rather than in the cloud, which lets us evolve our systems daily with tight feedback loops and quick development cycles.

  As a Production Engineer, you'll be responsible for deploying, maintaining, monitoring and improving the reliability, scalability and performance of our in-house built trading systems. Your scope covers market access, monitoring and compliance, strategy evaluation and management, performance tuning and trading automation. Sitting on our trading floor, you'll have constant face-to-face interaction with Traders, Researchers and fellow Engineers – this is an engineering role, not a support role, and you'll help set the standards for our production environment.
