We need your help on Saturday to refresh [Django](https://www.djangoproject.com/)’s marketing! We want to build a new website for the project. We’re working on a new marketing strategy to kickstart this work. We need help from newcomers bringing their fresh perspective. And seasoned Djangonauts. People who are new to marketing, or experienced. You can contribute with:

- Feedback on Django’s current marketing and website, ideas and brainstorming on what to do differently
- Collaborative research for specific aspects of our marketing strategy
- Curiosity, enthusiasm, ideas, criticisms
- Laptop or smartphone or paper and pen

Planning doc: [Sprint on Django (marketing) @ EuroPython 2026](https://bit.ly/django-ep2026)

Resources that will help: [Website redesign](https://github.com/django/djangoproject.com/issues/2287), [GitLab marketing handbook](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/marketing/), [Wagtail product strategy](https://wagtail.org/product-strategy), [Drupal product strategy](https://www.drupal.org/about/initiatives/cms/blog/drupal-cms-product-strategy-version-20), [WordPress marketing handbook](https://make.wordpress.org/marketing/handbook/), [Marketing open source projects](https://todogroup.org/resources/guides/marketing-open-source-projects/)

If you want to chat about / work on other things than marketing, come say hi! We will focus on the one theme but would love to hear what others are up to.
