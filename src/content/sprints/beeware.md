Ever wanted to write an app for your phone using nothing but Python? Ever wanted
to package your Python project so you can give it to someone else who knows
nothing about Python? You can - with BeeWare! We welcome new contributors; no
matter your level of experience, we can find a way for you to contribute. And
every new contributor earns a challenge coin!
