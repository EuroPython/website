**Organisers Meetup — Friday 17 July** Gather with fellow PyCon and PyO
organisers from across Europe. Share what's working, what isn't, and what's next
for your community.

**Community Lunch — Friday 17 July, 13:00–14:00** Sit down together over a
shared meal. An informal space to connect with peers, swap stories, and build
lasting relationships.

**Open Space Sessions — Friday 17 July, Afternoon** Unconference-style
discussions on running events, growing communities, diversity, sponsorship, and
everything in between.

**More activities to be announced** We're making this year's summit bigger than
EP2025. Stay tuned for additional programming.
