Volunteer at EuroPython #

EuroPython is run by volunteers. Every year, dozens of people help make the conference happen — and you can be one of them. Volunteering is in-person only and requires a conference ticket.

🎤
Session Chairing

Introduce speakers, keep talks on time, moderate Q&A sessions, and pass the microphone to the audience. A great way to meet speakers and be part of the programme.

🏢
Registration Desk

Welcome participants, hand out badges, check IDs for badge collection, and be the friendly first face people see when they arrive at the venue.

🧭
Room Manager

Monitor room capacity, assist with AV setup coordination, and support the Code of Conduct team in session rooms throughout the day.

👕
T-Shirt Handout

Help distribute volunteer and attendee T-shirts at the designated handout location, verifying sizes and eligibility.

🔧
Setup & Teardown

Help set up the venue before the conference begins and assist with teardown at the end. Includes signage, furniture arrangement, and general logistics.

🏃
Runners & Ad-Hoc

Flexible support wherever it's needed — from guiding attendees around the venue to helping the logistics team with last-minute tasks.

What you get
Free Combined Ticket

Volunteers who commit to 10 or more hours of shifts receive a complimentary combined conference ticket (tutorials + main conference).

Volunteer T-Shirt

A special edition EuroPython volunteer T-shirt so everyone knows you're one of the people making it all happen.

Website Credit & Group Photo

Your name listed on the EuroPython website and a mention in the closing session, plus the volunteer group photo.

Some English is required. You'll receive a full briefing before your shifts. Spaces are limited — apply early. Questions? Contact the volunteer team.
