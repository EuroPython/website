Visa Support #

Poland is in the Schengen Area. If you need a visa to attend EuroPython, we're here to help you through the process.

Do I need a visa?

EU/EEA citizens can enter Poland freely. Citizens of many other countries can enter visa-free for short stays. Check the Polish government visa page ↗ to see if your nationality requires a Schengen visa.

Invitation Letter

Once you've purchased your ticket, we can provide an official invitation letter to support your visa application. Fill out our invitation letter request form with your details and order confirmation.

Visa Fee Reimbursement

Through our Financial Aid programme, we offer reimbursement of up to €90 for Schengen visa fees. Apply for financial aid when applications open alongside ticket sales.

Tips for a smooth application
  • Apply early. Schengen visa processing can take 15–45 days. We recommend applying as soon as you have your ticket.
  • Book refundable accommodation. Many embassies require proof of accommodation. Book a refundable hotel so you can cancel if needed.
  • Bring your ticket confirmation. Your EuroPython ticket and invitation letter serve as proof of purpose of travel.
  • Travel insurance. Most Schengen visa applications require travel medical insurance with minimum €30,000 coverage.
